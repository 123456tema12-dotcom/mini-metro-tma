How to Play
Master the art of subway network planning

1. Draw Lines: Touch and drag from any station to another station to lay tracks.

2. Extend & Loop: Drag the circle handle at the end of a line to connect more stations, or back to the start to form a loop!

3. Insert Stations: Drag any line segment between two stations onto a third station to route trains through it.

4. Watch Overcrowding: Stations hold up to 6 passengers. If a station exceeds capacity, a warning timer starts ticking!

5. Sunday Upgrades: Survive until Sunday night to receive new trains, lines, tunnels, and interchanges.

6. Mobile Navigation: Use two fingers to pinch-to-zoom and pan across the city map.
